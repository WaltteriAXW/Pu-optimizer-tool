# Polyurethane Foam Product Selection Guide

## Product Overview

### Genfoam HD12
**Type:** High Density Pour/Mold Foam
**Density Range:** 350-550 kg/m³ (molded)
**Best For:**
- Pour-in-place applications
- Molded foam parts
- Applications requiring very high density
- Both high and low pressure machines

**Key Features:**
- Water-blown system
- Very high applied density capability
- Free rise density: 195-215 kg/m³
- 6-month shelf life (polyol should be re-mixed after 3 months)

---

### Genfoam HD20
**Type:** High Density Pour/Mold Foam
**Density Range:** 400-600 kg/m³ (molded)
**Best For:**
- Pour-in-place applications requiring maximum density
- Structural foam applications
- High-load bearing applications

**Key Features:**
- Water-blown system
- Highest density option in the range
- Free rise density: 290-315 kg/m³
- Similar processing to HD12 but denser output
- 6-month shelf life (polyol should be re-mixed after 3 months)

**Comparison to HD12:**
- ~50% higher free rise density
- Higher molded density range
- Similar processing conditions

---

### Ecomate Spray EC
**Type:** Spray Foam System
**Density:** 40±4 kg/m³ (applied)
**Best For:**
- Continuous spray coatings
- Thermal insulation applications
- Building insulation
- Applications requiring CE marking

**Key Features:**
- ecomate® blown (environmentally friendly)
- CE marked per EN 14315-1:2013
- Fire rating: Euroclass E, d0
- Excellent thermal properties (k-factor 0.019-0.022 W/m·K)
- >90% closed cell content
- Good adhesion to multiple substrates

**Environmental Benefits:**
- No GWP (Global Warming Potential)
- No ODP (Ozone Depletion Potential)
- PFAS-free
- Readily biodegradable
- Compliant with Montreal/Kyoto/Paris/Kigali protocols

**Application Requirements:**
- Layer thickness: 1-2.5 cm per pass
- Substrate humidity: ≤20% (porous), no condensation (nonporous)
- Fast reaction: 8-12s cream, 18-26s tack-free

---

### Ecofoam XHD RC
**Type:** Extra High Density Rigid Closed Cell Foam
**Density:** 40-45 kg/m³ (free rise)
**Best For:**
- Insulating panel production
- Cavity filling applications
- Discontinuous processes
- High compression strength requirements

**Key Features:**
- ecomate® blown (environmentally friendly)
- Exceptional compressive strength: 414 kPa parallel, 275 kPa perpendicular
- >95% closed cell content
- Fire rating: Euroclass E
- Excellent thermal properties (k-factor 0.019-0.022 W/m·K)
- Superior dimensional stability

**Environmental Benefits:**
- No GWP (Global Warming Potential)
- No ODP (Ozone Depletion Potential)
- PFAS-free
- Compliant with all major environmental protocols

**Processing:**
- Requires heated molds/substrates (35-45°C)
- Discontinuous process
- High and low pressure compatible

## Storage Requirements

All products require:
- Storage temperature: 10-25°C
- Protection from moisture
- Original sealed containers
- Dry storage location
- Protection from rain/snow
- No water accumulation near containers

**Special Notes:**
- Isocyanate components are moisture sensitive
- Genfoam polyols should be re-mixed after 3 months
- Ecomate Spray EC polyol has 3-month shelf life (shortest in range)

---

## Safety Considerations

All products require:
- Eye protection
- Gloves
- Proper ventilation
- Understanding of material hazards
- Compliance with all regulations
- Reference to Safety Data Sheets (SDS)

**Spray foam specific (Ecomate Spray EC):**
- Air-purifying respirator required
- Full body coverage (clean overalls)
- Safety glasses to prevent splash exposure
- Immediate glove removal if contaminated
